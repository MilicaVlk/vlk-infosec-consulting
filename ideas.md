# VLK InfoSec Consulting — Ground-Truth Brand Spec

## Reference status

This project follows the user-provided VLK InfoSec Consulting visual identity and presentation templates as the source of truth. The previous navy/cream consulting direction is discarded and must not be reintroduced.

## Visual language

The interface uses a deep near-black and midnight-blue base with flowing bands of royal blue, violet, magenta and pink. Metallic gold is the primary foreground accent for the VLK shield/merkaba mark, headings, frames and separators. Fine network lines, small nodes and angular geometry create the security and governance vocabulary. The visual tone is premium, dramatic, strategic and controlled.

## Owned motifs

The original shield/merkaba logo is the primary brand mark. The Direction · Structure · Assurance triangle is the central strategic motif. Thin gold frames, network diagrams, flowing wave bands and sharp geometric corners are recurring supporting elements drawn from the supplied cover and PowerPoint templates.

## Color tokens

The working tokens are extracted from the supplied color-reference artwork and matched against the visible templates: Midnight `#000030`, Deep Violet `#300050`, Violet `#6000C0`, Magenta `#D01060`, Pink Magenta `#E02060`, Royal Blue `#0050D0`, Gold `#D4A574`, and Soft Gold `#F2D39B`.

## Layout rules

The page is a dark editorial consulting site, not a light SaaS dashboard. Content bands alternate between full-width atmospheric surfaces and structured dark panels. Cards use thin gold or gradient borders rather than white surfaces. Headings are left-aligned. The triangle motif and linework should appear in the hero, the service model, the responsibility map and the footer.

## Typography rules

Typography must be bold, geometric and legible on dark backgrounds. The displayed brand lockup and template headings are the visual reference. Until an original font file is supplied, use a close geometric sans-serif web fallback and keep typography treatment consistent: bold uppercase labels with tracking for metadata, strong sentence-case headings, and compact readable body copy.

## Content hierarchy

The primary message is the vCISO model: Direction, Structure and Assurance. Supporting service areas are Strategic Guidance, Risk Management and Compliance Leadership. Core competencies are Technical Depth, Business Acumen and Communication Skills. Responsibility is organized as Owns, Oversees and Advises On.
