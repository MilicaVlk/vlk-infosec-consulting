# VLK InfoSec Consulting — Brand Audit

## Source materials

The visual system is based on the user-provided logo lockup, Cover01 banner, template board and embedded color-reference artwork from `clors.docx`.

## Confirmed visual language

The dominant base is near-black/deep midnight navy. The primary expressive gradient moves through royal blue, violet, magenta and electric pink. Gold is the premium foreground accent, used for the shield/merkaba logo, headings, rules, frames and key labels. Fine network lines and flowing fabric-like bands provide texture. The Direction · Structure · Assurance triangle is a core brand motif and must remain visible as a recurring structural element.

## Implementation rules

Use dark surfaces as the default, with gold typography and thin gold frames. Use magenta/violet/blue gradients as controlled atmosphere rather than generic backgrounds. Use white or soft-gold text on dark surfaces. Do not introduce cream cards, generic navy SaaS styling, unrelated illustrations, or alternative font pairings. The supplied logo lockup and cover are authoritative references; generated illustrations are not part of the final brand system.
